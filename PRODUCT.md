# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Portfolio reviewers and casual visitors who want to play a short, registration-free session while observing a server-controlled game. Their primary task is to spin, understand the outcome, and settle an in-game prize.

## Product Purpose

The Vault Algorithm demonstrates reliable backend game logic through a four-reel vintage slot-machine experience. The server owns outcomes, balances, prizes, history, and records; the browser presents those results clearly.

## Brand Personality

Mysterious, tactile, deliberate. It evokes a well-kept vintage machine, never a real-money betting product.

## Anti-references

Avoid contemporary betting-app cues, aggressive neon, generic SaaS panels, commercial pressure, and any suggestion of real-money gambling.

## Design Principles

- Make the machine feel physical while controls remain immediately legible.
- Make server-owned state understandable at the moment it changes.
- Keep costs, prizes, and rules visible and plain.
- Support a short, safe anonymous session without friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high contrast, visible focus states, keyboard support, text-based status feedback, touch targets of at least 44px where possible, and reduced-motion support.
